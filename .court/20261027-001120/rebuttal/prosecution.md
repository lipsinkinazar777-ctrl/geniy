# Prosecution rebuttal

1. **Does not survive.** "Low fixed cost" is unproven: stand rent, tax status, VAT and insurance are all absent, and so are capacity and hours. Defense itself calls the missing sales numbers a gap. A gap means nobody can say the business holds up.

2. **Does not survive.** Margin cannot be shown with no cost of goods, and the CRM cost fields are empty. "Quick to fill in" has not been done. Competitor mugs at 13.99-18 EUR set a ceiling, not proof of demand. The Prestige sets also carry invented crossed-out prices, and the file says set prices were never checked against cost.

3. **Partly survives.** The scripts exist, but nothing in the record shows they have ever run. The funnel has no payment, delivery or returns, and no legal notices. Pickup only at a weekend stand caps the 5-language online case. Selling "online" with no checkout and no delivery is not what the case describes.

4. **Does not survive.** The replies are in three languages, but the site has five. English, Ukrainian, Russian and Arabic are unchecked AI text, and the site has no separate URLs per language, so it cannot rank in search in any language. No evidence says customers need Arabic or English. Instagram shows 0 posts and 1 follower, and TikTok has no account.

5. **Does not survive.** Naming a risk does not fix it. The "Few customers at the market" problem is still open, and the Google listing may be refused. Defense concedes that nothing proves profit.

**The point for the jury:** point 2. Whether any set or product sells above its full cost, including the box, labour and stand rent. No cost is in the record, so no profit claim can be tested, and the case as stated depends on it.
