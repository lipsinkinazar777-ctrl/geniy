## Funnel / customer process (Voronka file and WhatsApp quick replies)
- 15 quick replies x 3 languages (FR/UK/RU) = 45 for WhatsApp Business: greeting, ask what/how many/when, prices, request photo, show mock-up, mock-up reminder (once), total and deposit ("acompte" via IBAN/link), order accepted, order ready for pickup at the stand, stand address/hours, story request next day (-10% for a story with a tag), Google review request after 3-4 days (no discount), pre-holiday reminder, win-back for people who wrote but did not order, offline message.
- Flow: first message -> product/quantity/date -> price -> photo -> free mock-up -> "OK" -> payment/deposit -> production -> pickup at stand (weekend) -> story request -> review request -> pre-holiday and win-back reminders.
- Five Instagram touchpoints designed: stand + card QR; first-message auto-reply with the Instagram link; "order ready" message with a small gift at the stand; at pickup show the QR and give a mini magnet for following (10 seconds); card in the bag with QR and -10% next order + next-day story request.
