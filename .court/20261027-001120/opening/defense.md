# Defense: the case should go ahead as described

1. **A real, low-fixed-cost operation already exists.** The file says: "a family workshop making gifts personalised with the customer's photos", with a stand "Saturday and Sunday 10:00-18:00", and "they print, engrave and assemble every gift themselves". Production is on demand ("sur commande"), so there is no stock risk. Objection: "no sales numbers". That is a gap in the record, not evidence of failure.

2. **Prices leave room for margin.** The author found competitors at "13.99 EUR (pariszigzag.fr) and 18 EUR (paris-friendly.fr)" for a photo mug, while the files price it at 10 EUR and suggest 12-14 EUR. The ladder runs from 2 EUR to a 66 EUR set, so there is an upsell path. Objection: "No cost of goods... appears anywhere". Mugs by sublimation and small metal items are low-cost goods, and the price-up route is documented. The missing costs are quick to fill in.

3. **A sales process is already designed.** There are "15 quick replies x 3 languages", a flow from first message to "pickup at stand -> story request -> review request -> pre-holiday and win-back reminders", and a deposit step. Objection: the site has no checkout. The WhatsApp cart with "free preview" before payment suits made-to-order goods and costs nothing to run.

4. **The audience and the languages fit the owners' real customers.** The audience is "mothers, fathers, grandparents, babies/births, couples... weddings, Ramadan/Eid", and the replies are in "French, Ukrainian and Russian". Objection: the AI-written translations are unchecked. The README itself says to have them checked, so this is a known to-do and not a flaw in the plan.

5. **The risks are already named by the owner's own files.** The files flag the misleading crossed-out prices, the Google Maps risk, "Few customers at the market" (High priority), and the missing legal pages. A plan that lists its own weak points can be fixed.

**Conceded:** nothing in the record proves profit. There are no costs, revenue, stand rent, tax status or VAT, and Instagram has "0 posts, 1 follower". The site also lacks delivery, returns and legal notices. These gaps mean the verdict must be conditional, not that the case should stop.
