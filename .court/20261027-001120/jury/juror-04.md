VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: The case asks for a verdict that the business will hold up and make a profit, but the record contains no cost, revenue, rent, tax or capacity figures, so that claim is hope, and the "online, 5-language" description does not match a WhatsApp-and-weekend-stand-pickup operation with no checkout, delivery or legal pages.
DECIDING FACT: "No cost of goods (blank mugs, metal plates, ink, laser time, packaging, labour) appears anywhere; the CRM product records have empty 'price' and 'cost' fields", with no sales numbers and Instagram at 0 posts and 1 follower, and the defense itself conceded that "nothing in the record proves profit".
WOULD FLIP IF: The record included real unit costs and some actual sales or stand-takings data showing the main products sell above full cost (including box, labour and stand rent), or the case were restated as a conditional, pre-launch assessment of a stand-and-WhatsApp business rather than a profitable 5-language online one.
